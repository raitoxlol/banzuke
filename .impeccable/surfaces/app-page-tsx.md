# Room broadcast

Scope is the main Banzuke room board. Mode is Operate. The visitor reads the lead, rankings, and latest plays at a glance, or scans the QR code to join.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A sumo competition broadcast whose tug-of-war score owns the first glance. The user's layout and dark sports-channel direction are binding. Avoid extra stat cards, decorative headings, and a map that competes with the match.

OWN-WORLD: A charcoal and pine-neutral ground, ivory type, ochre-orange Claude data, sage-mint Codex data, and red reserved for live status. Barlow Condensed provides broadcast numerals and the wordmark; Barlow provides readable UI text. Japanese ranks use a Mincho serif. Panels have quiet boundaries and consistent alignment.

STORY: First see which side leads and by how much. Then see each stable's rank and who just scored. Scan or open the corner QR to join. Private work reads as a private repo.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact full-width header contains the wordmark, #1J, status, JST clock and countdown, and a 112px QR. Below it, two large side scores flank a modest VS and lead label. The animated rope spans the board. The lower area divides roughly 56/44 into six stable rows and the plays feed. A single scrolling commentary strip closes the screen. The signature interaction is the rope sliding toward the leader while stable rows move to their new places. Reduced-motion mode preserves positions without animation.

FORM: The third grounded direction is a contemporary sumo banzuke adapted to a broadcast screen. Impeccable seed dd420d33 assigned index 3. The seven considered worlds were stadium graphics, Japanese timetable typography, sumo ranking sheets, jazz-program tables, racing-result cards, Tokyo street wayfinding, and exhibition-result placards. The user already specifies the composition and subject. Challenger disciplines improve this direction without changing that contract: orienteering contributes a consistent legend for both sides; monochrome product design contributes restraint around the data; streaming catalogs contribute explicit focus and selected states; the garden-map palette is declined because it obscures rank and scoring hierarchy. Jet-age ticket wallets and Datamatics remain in the re-roll pool; no identity elements were borrowed from them.
